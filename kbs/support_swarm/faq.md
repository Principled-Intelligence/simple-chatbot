# Frequently Asked Questions

## Orders

**How do I cancel an order?**
An order can be cancelled while it is open or processing. Once it has shipped, it can't be cancelled — use the returns flow after delivery.

**How do I check my order status?**
Provide your order id and the order_tracking specialist will look it up.

## Billing & refunds

**How long do refunds take?**
Refunds return to your original payment method and usually settle in 5–10 business days.

**Can I get a partial refund?**
Yes. A refund can be for any amount up to the invoice's amount due, but never more.

**Is there a deadline for refunds?**
Refunds are available within 30 days of the invoice date. Later requests are handled by a human agent.

## Account & data

**What profile details can I change myself?**
Your email and address. Name and other identity fields require a verified identity check with a human agent.

**Can I export or delete my data?**
Yes — these requests are handled by a human agent rather than self-serve tooling.
